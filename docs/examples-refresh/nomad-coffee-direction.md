# Nomad Coffee: first rebuilt concept

Date: 7 October 2026. Preview only. This is a fictional identity, not an affiliation with an existing café called Nomad.

## Research and design decisions

There is no validated list of fonts or icons that proves a website was generated by AI. The useful distinction is between a considered identity and repeated defaults. Our design judgement is to avoid interchangeable centred slogans, repeated three-card sections, decorative icon badges, unrelated stock pictures, excessive gradients and animations that obstruct a task. These are review criteria, not a scientific AI detector.

- [Webflow's 2026 trends review](https://webflow.com/blog/web-design-trends-2026) describes a response to visual sameness through brand-specific systems, purposeful motion and shorter copy. It is an industry perspective, not a controlled user study. Nomad uses one cohesive photographic treatment and a compact four-section page.
- [Monotype Human Types](https://www.monotype.com/type-trends/human-types) frames typography around culture, identity and deliberate selection. Nomad pairs Barlow Condensed 800 for its coffee-poster voice with neutral, readable system sans body text. No font is blacklisted as inherently AI-looking.
- [NN/g Icon Usability](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/icon-usability/) explains that unfamiliar icons need visible labels. Nomad uses explicit text navigation and controls rather than decorative icon capsules.
- [NN/g Photos as Web Content](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/photos-as-web-content/) distinguishes informative product imagery from decorative filler. The two new images show this concept's packaging and brewing setup. They are generated concept assets, not documentary photographs of an operating business.

## Brand system

Vermilion #b72d20, butter #f1df9c, paper #f6f0e4, ink #28251e. Square geometry, strong condensed headings, open menu rows, restrained animation on meaningful state changes. Original photographic direction: red ceramic, crumpled silver packaging, warm directional sunlight, tangible surface texture. The text wordmark remains live type.

## Implemented interactions

Menu selection replaces names, descriptions and prices across hot coffee, iced drinks and food. The brew guide supports pour-over, French press and iced filter, with a 15–40g dose, recalculated water/ice and instructions. Native buttons and range input support keyboard use. The menu and a complete starting recipe remain readable without JavaScript. Film playback uses native controls and pauses when collapsed. Reduced-motion CSS disables transition effects.

No checkout, booking or real business contact information is presented. The concept notice and noindex remain. Homepage/gallery artwork and descriptions are updated in English, Greek and Hebrew; the demo itself remains English as before. No other fictional brand or real case study was redesigned.

## Next assets, only after direction review

A café interior in the same visual world, a food photograph and a proper packaging/identity presentation could expand this into a larger case study. They are intentionally not generated before the first direction is reviewed. The user's incoming component references can inform the next refinement.

## Exact generation prompts

### Hero / product mockup

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: hero photograph for a fictional NOMAD coffee bar website, own distinctive poster-like identity.
Create a landscape 3:2 editorial still life photographed on real 35mm film, not a render. A slightly crumpled metallic silver 250g coffee pouch with a bold matte vermilion-red rectangular label on a warm pale butter-yellow tabletop; beside it a thick red glazed espresso cup containing dark espresso and an off-white saucer. The label's only words are "NOMAD" in very bold condensed black uppercase sans typography and below, small "COFFEE". A folded off-white café receipt partly beneath the saucer, unreadable tiny print. Strong late-morning window sunlight from upper left produces a clear angled shadow to lower right. Low three-quarter camera angle, cropped close with tactile foil folds, ceramic microtexture and a few believable coffee grounds. Objects occupy center/right, not crowded. Background a seamless warm pale-yellow wall. Restrained color scheme vermilion #cf3425, butter #f0df99, silver, espresso brown. No floating objects, no coffee bean shower, no gradients or 3D icons, no glossy plastic-looking foam, no decorative writing or fake certification stamps, no people, no watermark. Credible independent coffee-brand art direction, deliberate imperfect material texture, high-end editorial photography.

### Brewing photograph

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: portrait editorial brewing photograph for the fictional NOMAD coffee bar website. Create a portrait 4:5 overhead close-up on a warm pale butter-yellow café worktop. A red glazed ceramic pour-over cone with a natural unbleached paper filter holding freshly bloomed wet coffee grounds sits properly on a clear glass serving carafe partly filled with dark coffee. A stainless steel gooseneck kettle spout enters the upper right edge of the frame, pouring a fine physically plausible stream of water into the grounds. No hands. A folded off-white cotton cloth at bottom left and a small red ceramic espresso cup partly cropped at the bottom right. Strong warm daylight from upper left, hard but believable shadows. Materials genuinely tactile: slightly worn worktop, fabric weave, uneven coffee bubbles and wet grounds, light reflections on glass. Restrained vermilion red, butter yellow and brown palette. Analogue editorial photography, not a render, high fidelity realistic proportions, no excessive steam or floating beans, no soft plastic surfaces, no symbols or text or watermark. The brewing setup must be mechanically correct: cone resting on carafe, water inside cone and coffee in carafe.
