# Batch B: before and after

Pricing, Contact and FAQ, Greek and Hebrew. Written to the voice guide as it stands after the owner's Batch A feedback: full conversational sentences, plain words, a few real expressions, nothing clipped.

## What did not change

- **Every price, currency token and plan name.** A script compared every € amount in the six changed files before and after: identical. The regional-currency feature rewrites these tokens, so their exact form matters.
- **What each plan includes, revision rounds, page limits, payment terms, cancellation notice, ownership terms, the "we don't guarantee Google rankings" line.** Reworded where stiff, never changed in meaning.
- **The approved Enterprise wording** "Τιμή προσαρμοσμένη στο εύρος του έργου σας" / "מחיר מותאם להיקף הפרויקט" (a test guards it).
- **Google titles and descriptions** for these pages (Batch F).
