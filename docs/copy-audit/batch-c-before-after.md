# Batch C: before and after

Services overview, Process, and the 9 service pages (custom design, mobile, speed, SEO, security, delivery, maps, forms, social), Greek and Hebrew. Same voice as Batches A and B: full conversational sentences, plain words, a few real expressions per page, none in FAQ answers.

## What did not change

- **Facts and numbers.** Revision rounds (2 / 3 / 4), page limits (up to 4 / up to 7), the 15–20 minute call, 30 days of bug fixing, "paid in full before work begins", the timeline agreed per project. A script compared these counts in every changed file before and after.
- **Every "we don't guarantee" line.** No Google position, no Lighthouse score, no uninterrupted uptime, no universal recovery time, no legal compliance from consent tools alone. Reworded, never removed.
- **Prices.** No € amounts in these files changed.
- **Google titles and descriptions** (Batch F). Exception: the Greek Maps, Forms and Social pages build their Google title from the page heading, so those three titles now read "Google Maps και τοποθεσία | DM-Labs.io" and so on.

## Greek self-review

Checked before pushing:

- **"Convert X into Y" calques:** none left on these pages (they were on Maps, Forms and Social).
- **English idioms:** "έχετε ήδη χάσει τη μάχη" removed.
- **Form of address:** one generic "εσύ" slipped into my own draft ("καταλαβαίνεις") and was changed to "καταλαβαίνει κανείς". No other "εσύ".
- **Pronouns with nothing to refer to:** "πριν ξεκινήσουμε να τη φτιάχνουμε" (build *it*, with no "it" in the sentence) now names the website.
- **Ambiguous "σας":** "Σε κάθε στάδιο σας ρωτάμε" could read as "your stage". Now "ζητάμε τη γνώμη σας".
- **Accent rule:** a script checked every word before σας / μας / του for the second accent Greek needs ("συντήρησής σας", "όριά του"). No errors.
- **Repetition:** the same idea three times on the Maps page ("να σας βρίσκουν") cut to one. Two "πρώτα" in a row removed.
- **A verbless "sentence"** in the mobile principles now has a verb.
