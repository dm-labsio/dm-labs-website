# Batch E: Greek city pages and blog

Same method as the Greek redo: facts come only from the English pages and the shared Pricing and FAQ copy, then every line is written fresh in Greek and read aloud. Country names stay out of the copy (`server/internationalPositioning.test.ts`); city names are fine.

## Part 1: the five city pages (Cyprus, Limassol, Nicosia, Thessaloniki, Crete)
