# Pricing UX review

29 September 2026. Scope: make the existing plans easier to understand and turn the pricing page into a useful decision tool. Prices, scope, ownership and payment terms are not being redesigned.

## Findings on the previous page

The three full-width rows forced visitors to remember each package while scrolling. Broad descriptions did little to distinguish the packages. Selecting a build immediately scrolled away from the comparison. The combined build and recurring care budget was not calculated. Mobile comparison rows and FAQs hid most answers behind closed disclosures.

## Research and application

- [NN/g: Comparison Tables for Products, Services, and Features](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/comparison-tables/) recommends consistent comparable attributes, short values and an easily scanned layout for a small set of alternatives. Applied here as three aligned desktop cards, matching page/revision facts, explicit inheritance between packages, and a feature comparison with a differences-only filter. These are design principles, not evidence that this particular redesign increases conversion.
- [Framer pricing](https://www.framer.com/pricing) puts a short intended-use statement beside each tier and separates the overview from deeper comparison. Applied here as concrete use cases for a simple business introduction, enquiries, or a portfolio/content-rich site, followed by complete scope information.
- [Linear pricing](https://linear.app/pricing) uses concise tier highlights and explicit billing labels, with deeper feature comparison below. Applied here as clearer one-time versus recurring labels, visible annual totals, and a selection summary that stays available while comparing.

## Chosen interaction model

An optional three-goal selector suggests a starting plan and explains why. It never silently selects or purchases a plan. The result includes its build price and required recurring care before the visitor chooses it; that explicit action continues to care. Visitors can also compare and select the plan cards directly, without being scrolled away.

A persistent summary shows the build and care choices. A first-year estimate adds the build once and either twelve monthly payments or one annual payment. Its nearby explanation distinguishes budgeting from payment timing and states the tax/domain/third-party exclusions. Changes are reflected immediately, with a reset control and the existing localized consultation handoff.

Motion is limited to choice feedback, a short recommendation entrance and the changing estimate. No autoplay media, perpetual animation, extra dependency, forced quiz or hidden essential pricing. Dark supplied artwork, large brand numerals and restrained cyan/lavender selection states provide the visual character. Phone layouts avoid wide tables and keep disclosures open initially.

## Practical limits

Browser verification can confirm calculations, layout, keyboard behavior and the consultation flow. It does not establish a conversion improvement or replace testing with real prospective customers. After the direction is approved, useful follow-up measures would be successful package selection, consultation starts and where visitors hesitate. No analytics changes or experiments were deployed in this checkpoint.
