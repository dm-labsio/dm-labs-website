ARCOS concept showcase / 7 October 2026

Interaction research:
Aceternity Lens by Manu Arora, discovered through the 21st.dev collection:
https://21st.dev/community/components/explore/parallax-components
https://ui.aceternity.com/components/lens

Aceternity Tracing Beam:
https://ui.aceternity.com/components/tracing-beam

These are interaction references. The native image magnifier, drawing reveal,
reading-progress line, lightbox and project-brief builder are original DOM/CSS
implementations. No Aceternity source or paid component is redistributed.
The lens adds explicit material selectors for touch and keyboard access;
reduced motion disables cursor tracking but retains static magnification.

Imagery: three original AI-generated architectural concept views, created
with the built-in image_gen tool. These depict a fictional, unbuilt project.
The plan is a schematic illustration, not a measured construction drawing.
Fonts: Syne and IBM Plex Sans, served by Google Fonts.
Asset prompts are recorded in docs/examples-refresh/arcos-assets.json.
