Sunday Boat website demo
All brand artwork, photographs and font files are from the user-supplied
Sunday-Boat-Web-Designer-Handoff.zip (9 October 2026). Original assets retained
outside the repository. The supplied 768px/full WebP exports are used unchanged.
SVG wordmarks/symbols are the supplied vector masters. Fonts carry bundled OFL
licenses. Cobalt/white/pistachio follows brand tokens; red is not used in this page.

Photography is supplied concept imagery for a fictional portfolio brand.
No real restaurant address, opening hours, reservations, payment processing,
shopping cart or e-commerce is represented. Menu dishes follow the supplied
04-menu/menu.html. Prices omitted because currency and prices are unconfirmed.

Interaction reference: Card Fan Carousel by Aayush Duhan, 21st.dev
https://21st.dev/@aayush-duhan/components/card-fan-carousel
Reviewed 9 October 2026. Fanned layout and card-selection concept inspired the
hero. Implementation is original CSS/JavaScript, not copied component source;
no GSAP or third-party runtime is loaded. Ticket buttons, paper menu, package
reveal and merchandise clothesline are original brand-specific implementations.
