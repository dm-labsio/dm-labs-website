Pulse Gym interaction edition, October 2026.

Existing Pulse identity retained: Barlow Condensed / Barlow, black and yellow palette, gym photography and section order. The existing Unsplash photographs remain illustrative stock imagery. Coaches, memberships and premises are fictional. No live contact, payment or booking is performed.

Three native DOM/CSS adaptations of SmoothUI components by Eduardo Calvo, discovered through the SmoothUI library on 21st.dev:
https://21st.dev/@educalvolpz/library/smoothui

Animated Tabs
https://21st.dev/@educalvolpz/components/animated-tabs
https://smoothui.dev/r/animated-tabs.json
Adaptation: sliding rectangular yellow indicator, seven-day schedule, roving tabindex and left/right/Home/End keyboard behavior.

Magnetic Button
https://smoothui.dev/docs/components/magnetic-button
https://smoothui.dev/r/magnetic-button.json
Adaptation: distance/radius falloff on primary trial buttons, native pointer events, touch/reduced-motion opt-out, bounded movement without overlapping hit areas.

Tilt Card
https://smoothui.dev/docs/components/tilt-card
https://smoothui.dev/r/tilt-card.json
Adaptation: normalized pointer coordinates, perspective tilt and soft yellow glare on coach cards. Click opens a keyboard-accessible profile and leads to the filtered timetable. Reduced maximum tilt, no device motion sensor access.

Code adaptations live in motion.mjs, styling in pulse.css. React/Motion replaced with native browser events and CSS transitions. MIT license retained below.
