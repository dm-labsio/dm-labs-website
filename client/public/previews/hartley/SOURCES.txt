These public descriptions and usage examples informed original vanilla HTML/CSS/JavaScript adaptations. No upstream component implementation copied, no unverified source licence assumed, and no React page-flip runtime installed. The menu uses a short perspective page transition; it is not a simulation of a physical bound book. The arc and two-image slideshow were replaced after feedback with a kinetic typographic hero and floating imagery. Characters rotate into coffee, cake and company, with cursor/touch depth and an automatic floating fallback. The full sentence remains accessible as a fixed heading label. The page uses original straight-edged, double-rule buttons with rolling labels and understated underline motion. Reference descriptions informed the interactions; component source was not copied.

This standalone demo sends no orders, reservations, messages or payments. Menu prices are illustrative; downloaded tea artwork is the supplied concept menu. No loyalty offer activated. Reduced-motion alternatives, keyboard controls and touch-friendly actions are included. The complete café photographs are non-clickable and move gently on scroll. There are no image dialogs.

Three matching tea-selection photographs (tea-breakfast.webp, tea-grey.webp, tea-mint.webp) were generated using the built-in image_gen tool on 9 October 2026. The supplied tea-pour image was the style/location reference. Subjects: English Breakfast with scones, Earl Grey with lemon drizzle cake, Garden Mint with cucumber sandwiches. Original PNG files and complete prompts are preserved in workspace output/hartley-tea. Only optimized WebP derivatives ship to the website.
