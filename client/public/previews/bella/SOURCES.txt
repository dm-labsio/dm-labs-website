Gallery: native DOM/CSS adaptation of SmoothUI Hover Expand by Eduardo Calvo.
Discovered: https://21st.dev/@educalvolpz/components/hover-expand
Inspected source: https://smoothui.dev/r/hover-expand.json
Documentation: https://smoothui.dev/docs/components/hover-expand
Adaptation: client/public/previews/bella/hover-expand.mjs and the hair-gallery CSS.
Retains flex expansion, pointer/focus selection and left/right keyboard navigation.
Changes: equal-panel resting state, smaller expansion ratio, gallery/contact-sheet toggle, mobile two-column gallery, full-image lightbox, touch swipe and appointment handoff. Replaces React/Motion with native events and CSS transitions; respects reduced motion.
