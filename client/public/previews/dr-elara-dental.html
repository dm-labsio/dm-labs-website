Root canal treatment,
explained simply.

Root canal treatment removes infected tissue from inside a tooth. The canals are cleaned, filled and sealed. A filling or crown then restores the tooth.

The approach depends on the tooth and a dentist’s assessment. This film is an illustration, not an individual treatment plan.
